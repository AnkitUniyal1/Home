About Me

Turning complexity
into clarity

I'm a Data & Business Analyst based in Chandigarh, India — an insights architect who builds bridges between raw data and actionable strategies. I enjoy turning messy, complex datasets into clear stories that help people make smarter decisions.

My path into data isn't conventional. I spent two years as an Assistant Manager at Sai Enterprises, where I naturally gravitated toward building Excel dashboards and reports to make sense of inventory and sales data. That curiosity led me deeper — into Python, SQL, and formal analytics training at MedTourEasy.

Today I'm skilled in SQL, Python, Excel, Power BI, and Tableau, certified as a Python Data Associate (DataCamp) and holding a Google Data Analytics Professional Certificate, with tracks in Python Data Fundamentals and the Data Analyst track.

I'm actively looking for opportunities where I can contribute meaningful analysis, grow alongside strong teams, and keep solving problems with data.
